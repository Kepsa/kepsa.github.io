About me

I'm a WINQ research fellow in the Nordita institute in Stockholm (WINQ, The Wallenberg Initiative on Networks and Quantum Information).

Before that I had a postdoc position in the University of Aberdeen (2019-2012) and in the KTH Royal Institute of Technology (2021-2023). I obtained my PhD from Tampere University in 2019.

Research interests

  • homology and homotopy theories for (di)graphs
  • applied topology and persistent homology
  • applications to network neuroscience
